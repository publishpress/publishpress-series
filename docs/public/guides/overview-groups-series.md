# Series Categories

Table of Contents

- [How to use Series Categories](#0-toc-title)
- [How to Display the Categories](#how-to-display-the-groups)

[PublishPress Series](https://publishpress.com/links/series) allows you to organize individual posts together into a series. The plugin has a feature called Series Categories that gives you the ability to put series together in groups.

## How to use Series Categories [#](#0-toc-title)

Let’s take a look at a common use-case for Series Categories. This screenshot below is taken from a monthly publication. The Series Categories are years, such as “2020”, “2021” and “2022″.

![Series Categories Overview](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/series-categories-overview-8b91773ac9.webp)

*Series Categories Overview*

Inside each Series Category are individual series. In the example of this monthly magazine, each month has its own series. “January 2022”, “February 2022”, and “March 2022” are all placed inside the Series Category called “2022”.

![Series Categories](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/series-categories-73527e5c93.webp)

*Series Categories*

## How to Display the Categories [#](#how-to-display-the-groups)

[PublishPress Series](https://publishpress.com/links/series) arrives with a default layout for your categories, as you can see in the image below. This example uses a “Series Category” of “Star Wars”. The individual series are “Star Wars Originals”, “Star Wars Prequels” and “Star Wars Sequels”.

![Groups Overview](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/groups-overview-d777d5eb23.webp)

*Groups Overview*

You can customize this display by going to Series > Settings > Series Category. You can choose a custom layout for Series Categories by choosing “Use template from Theme” option. After changing that option, follow these steps to create your own layout:

- Copy the file `wp-content/plugins/organize-series/addons/grouping/theme/primary/taxonomy-series_group.php`
- Copy the file into your theme or child theme’s folder.
- Customize the HTML to match the design of your theme.

![Series Categories settings](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/series-categories-setting-0d67fba1d5.webp)
