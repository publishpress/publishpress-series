# The Manage Series Screen

Table of Contents

- [The Series Order Feature](#h-the-series-order-feature)
- [Publish or schedule posts](#1-toc-title)

The Manage Series feature is available in the Free and Pro versions of [PublishPress Series](https://publishpress.com/links/series). This feature allows you to quickly manage all posts linked to a Series. You can use this screen to re-order, schedule publish, and unpublish all the posts in a Series.

This “Manage Series” screen is available under the “Posts” link in the WordPress admin area:

In the image below, you can see the full “Manage Series” screen with 3 series:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/manage-series-screen-56167a2225.webp)

*Manage Series*

Here are some of the tools on this screen that you can use to manage your series:

- **Series order**: This allows you to change the order of the posts in your series.
- **Publish or schedule posts**: Either publish the whole series now, or schedule it for the future.
- **View series in frontend**: Takes you to the visitors’ view of this series.

## The Series Order Feature [#](#h-the-series-order-feature)

If you click on “Series order” you will be taken to the screen shown in the image below. This feature allows you to drag-and-drop posts in the Series into a new order. Take a look at the image below and you’ll see that movies are in wrong order (if you don’t like Star Wars, you’ll have to trust us on this).

![Series order screen](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/series-order-c6453a15e6.webp)

*Update Order Series*

You can select each row and drag and post into it’s correct position:

![Drag and drop series](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/series-order-reordered-693425097e.webp)

*Drag Drop Series*

Also on this screen, you can see any unpublished posts in this series. When you’re ready, you can click the “Publish Unpublished Posts” button to add them to the series.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/unpublished-posts-48d294938b.webp)

## Publish or schedule posts [#](#1-toc-title)

The “Publish or schedule posts” link will also take you to a new screen. In the right sidebar, you can choose a publishing date for any unpublished posts in this series.

![Publish All](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/publish-all-402b5616a7.webp)

*Publish All*

If you choose the current date, the posts will publish immediately. If you choose a date in the future, all the posts in the series will be scheduled. In the image below, you can see that 3 posts in this series are scheduled for future publication.

![Schedule All Posts](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-series/schedule-all-posts-f7677811e6.webp)
